## 📌 Overview

Mental health is one of the most under-addressed public health challenges, and access to care varies widely between regions, age groups and genders. This project turns raw mental healthcare data into a **single-page, interactive dashboard** that helps answer one core question:

> **Who needs mental healthcare, where, and how large is the gap between those who need treatment and those who receive it?**

The dashboard covers **2018 – 2025** across **18+ Asian countries** and **5 sub-regions**, giving decision-makers, researchers and NGOs a fast way to spot where support is most needed.

## 🎯 Key Features

| Feature | Description |
|---|---|
| 📊 **KPI Cards** | Treatment Gap, Care Percent and Total Population at a glance |
| 📅 **Year Slicer** | Filter the entire dashboard by year (2018 – 2025) |
| 🚻 **Gender Analysis** | Donut chart showing Female / Male / Non-binary distribution |
| 🎂 **Age Group Analysis** | Pie chart across six age bands: 15-24, 25-34, 35-44, 45-54, 55-64, 65+ |
| 🌏 **Country Comparison** | Stacked column chart ranking countries, broken down by age group |
| 📈 **Regional Trends** | Area chart comparing regions by gender |
| 🔻 **Regional Funnel** | Funnel visual ranking Southeast, West, South, Central and East Asia |
| 🎨 **Custom Design** | Cohesive teal, pink and purple theme with a clean, readable layout |

## 🖼️ Dashboard Walkthrough

### 1. KPI Summary
Three headline metrics at the top give an instant read on the situation:
- **Treatment Gap**: 50.72K
- **Care Percent**: 69.3K
- **Total Population**: 16.97K

### 2. Demographic Breakdown
- **Gender donut chart** shows how cases split between Female, Male and Non-binary groups.
- **Age pie chart** shows which age bands make up the largest share of the population.

### 3. Country-Level Insights
A stacked column chart ranks countries (Saudi Arabia, Turkmenistan, Japan, Kuwait, Bhutan, Iran, Pakistan, Thailand, Vietnam and more) with each bar split by age group, so you can see both **volume** and **who is affected**.

### 4. Regional Insights
- The **area chart** tracks the regional pattern from Southeast Asia down to East Asia, split by gender.
- The **funnel chart** ranks regions: **Southeast Asia (346)**, **West Asia (321)**, **South Asia (215)**, **Central Asia (180)** and **East Asia (138)**.

---

## 🔄 Project Workflow

```
Raw Data  →  Data Cleaning (Power Query)  →  Data Modeling  →  DAX Measures  →  Visualization  →  Insights
```

1. **Collect**: gathered mental healthcare data by country, region, age group and gender
2. **Clean**: handled missing values, fixed data types and standardised country and region names
3. **Model**: built relationships between fact and dimension tables
4. **Measure**: wrote DAX for Treatment Gap, Care Percent and Total Population
5. **Visualise**: designed an interactive, themed single-page report
6. **Interpret**: drew insights to support awareness and planning

---

## 💡 Key Insights

- 🌏 **Southeast Asia and West Asia** record the highest values, making them priority regions for mental health programs.
- 📉 **East Asia** shows the lowest figure among the five regions in this dataset.
- 👥 Age distribution is spread across all six groups, showing that mental health needs span **every stage of life**, not just one demographic.
- 🚻 Gender representation shows a **near-balanced split** between Female and Male, with a small Non-binary segment.
